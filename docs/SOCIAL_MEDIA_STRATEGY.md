# Keep Social Media Strategy — phone-first creation

Last updated: November 18, 2026

## Brand foundation
- Promise: **Some things are worth keeping.**
- Give people the emotional reason to create a Keep first. Show how easy it is second.
- Never imply the app only works on smartphones. Both computers and tablets remain supported.
- Keep language inviting and specific, not technically apologetic or repetitive.

## Channel and cadence
- Primary launch channel: Instagram (@mykeepcard) through Metricool.
- Preserve the established three-posts-per-week cadence at 7:30 p.m. Eastern while the current calendar runs.
- Introduce **one phone-first creation suggestion every 5–7 feed posts**, integrated into a useful how-to or recording demonstration.
- Supplement with occasional Stories showing how to tap a card and create a Keep on a phone, when production-ready imagery is available. A Story can link directly to Keep without teaching QR scanning to an audience already on phones.
- Don't force phone-first messaging into emotional tributes, recipient stories or every price/purchase post.

## Repeatable formats
1. **The memories are already there**: a close-up of a smartphone photo library transitioning into Keep's photo picker, with permission-cleared or product-only visuals.
2. **Record it in your voice**: a real phone screen recording showing voice capture and the simple, reassuring recording experience.
3. **Three steps to a Keep**: choose the person, add your words and media from your phone, give them a card that opens the experience.
4. **From laptop to phone**: show the subtle website QR prompt and how scanning opens Keep. This is educational content primarily for desktop visitors and can be a website demo reel.

## Suggested hooks
- “The best place to start? The photos already on your phone.”
- “The right words don't have to be perfect. Record them in your own voice.”
- “Start with a memory. Finish with something they'll keep forever.”
- “Creating a Keep on your phone is simple: your photos, your voice, their forever keepsake.”

## Calls to action
- “Create your Keep from your phone — link in bio.”
- “Open Keep on your phone and start with someone you love.”
- “Save this for when you're ready to tell the story.”
- Avoid linking straight to protected draft state or implying that unsaved uploads automatically follow across devices.

## Editorial guardrails
- Keep 80–85% of feed posts focused on personal stories, meaning, occasions, voice and product; 15–20% on practical how-to education.
- Within those how-to posts, mention phone-first creation occasionally (rather than in all of them).
- Prefer real product, screen captures and card mockups. Avoid AI-generated people in new creative.
- Respect the Basic $79 / Signature $99 current launch pricing if pricing is mentioned.
- Do not claim that scanning the website QR transfers local unsaved files. It opens the signed-in app; users must finish saving/syncing media first.

## Scheduled-calendar application (November–December 2026)
- Nov 30 voice-recording post: add one small sentence about recording naturally with a smartphone.
- Dec 11 year-in-review post: add one small sentence inviting readers to look through memories on their phones.
- Preserve all original artwork, times, core captions and hashtags for these edits.
- Do not add duplicate feed posts solely to repeat the phone recommendation.

## Success metrics
- Track visits to the Keep website from social bio, new creator sign-ins, creation starts, photo/video uploads and completion.
- Once implemented, measure desktop-to-phone handoffs as an aggregate funnel metric; don't treat mere QR impressions as completed conversions.
