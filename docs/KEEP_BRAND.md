# Keep — approved brand identity v1.1

Approved direction: Concept #1, light editorial luxury.

## Primary brand assets
- `public/brand/keep-wordmark.svg`: actual Concept #1 wordmark silhouette traced from the approved image. Use this shared asset, not typed "keep" or an improvised font logo.
- `public/brand/keep-monogram.svg`: matching approved K monogram.
- `public/favicon.svg`: the K monogram reversed on black.
- In app UI, `.brand` and `.keep-wordmark` use the SVG as a CSS mask so the same shape is black on light surfaces and ivory on black physical cards/film screens. The accessible text content must remain `Keep`.

## Brand palette
- Keep Black: `#0B0B0B` — logo, primary text, primary actions, NFC card.
- Warm Ivory: `#F7F5F2` — background.
- Soft Sand: `#E6DED3` — gentle panels, selected backgrounds.
- Warm Taupe: `#B8A999` — subtle accent/decorative material.
- Stone: `#4A4A45` — secondary brand tone.
- Taupe Ink: `#6A5849` — accessible text/icon color inspired by Warm Taupe.
- Borders: `#D8D0C7`.

## Type
- Wordmark: always the SVG, always title-case Keep (never KEEP/keep as the brand logo).
- Display/headlines: Playfair Display 400, sentence case; ~-0.035em tracking.
- Body/buttons/navigation: DM Sans 400–600, sentence case.
- Hero title: ~42px mobile, ~64px on large website pages.
- Section titles: ~30px mobile, ~42px large.
- Body: 16px, line-height 1.6.
- Buttons: 14px, medium weight.

## App presentation
- Default app, account, creation, "What is Keep?", saved creations: **light** backgrounds with dark text.
- Primary buttons: black with ivory text. Secondary cards: warm ivory/cream.
- Selection: clear dark border and soft sand fill.
- Creation editor: a dedicated dark cinematic workspace, including top bar, timeline, editing panels, and bottom tools; photo/video preview stays dark. The rest of the creation experience is warm ivory.
- Recipient playback and physical black NFC card remain dark; reverse the brand mask to ivory.
- No decorative gold gradients, saturated accent colors, or all-caps logotypes.

## Copy
- Brand name: Keep.
- Tagline: “Some things are worth keeping” (no final period in logo lockup).
- Mission headline: “Say what matters, keep it forever.”
